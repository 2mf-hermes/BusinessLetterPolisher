Keep the provider menu in place and collapse the connection block

The AI provider/model dropdowns still jumped out of view after they opened:
.cs-dd animated `all`, so csPlaceMenu() measured the START of the transition
and the self-correction pushed the menu off screen. Only opacity animates now,
placement freezes the transitions while measuring, the correction is bounded,
and a menu opened during the settings-panel slide is re-placed once the slide
settles.

The AI URL / key / model / temperature fields now stay hidden until Add or
Edit opens them, with a header, even row spacing and an active state on the
edit button.
